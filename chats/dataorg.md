# Data Organization

Broman and Woo (2018) is excellent ground-floor organizing of data. Very important.

Other important aspects:

* Metadata: information describing the origin of data including
  * experimental design
  * Data measurement process
  * Special cases or situations (temp, issues, etc.)
  * provenance of data (what steps lead to this data sheet?)
  * explanation of column names
* organizing data at scale
  * Efficient format for machine-reading
  * RDS and FST for R analysis pipelines
  * Parquet for broader audience
* Organization above the single spreadsheet level
  * Folders and READMEs
  * Connection to code pipelines
  * Written protocols
    * For within-team alignment
    * For sharing with public
* Backups and portability
  * Access by full team
  * Access to public

## References

* [Broman, K. W., & Woo, K. H. (2018). Data Organization in Spreadsheets. The American Statistician, 72(1), 2–10. (DOI: 10.1080/00031305.2017.137598)](https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2017.1375989)
